01 — Notre savoir-faire

The slow art of lamination

A true croissant can’t be hurried. Ours begin with cold butter folded into the dough by hand, then turned, rested, and turned again — the traditional French lamination that builds those delicate, honeycombed layers.

Nothing is rushed and nothing is faked. Each morning the ovens are lit and every tray is baked fresh, so what reaches the counter is crisp at the edges, tender at the heart and still fragrant with butter.

It is a quiet, old-fashioned way of working. We think you can taste the difference.
